About BWGL Group

Pioneering Premium Eco-Cleaning Solutions

BWGL Group places the client at the center of our services. We deliver an excellent two-pronged solution: premium cleaning/hygiene service systems and custom eco-friendly product distribution.

Based in the picturesque mountainous area of Heidelberg in Gauteng, we partner with corporate, business, public sector, and retail clients to deliver top-tier hygienic solutions. Through our foundation, we run community outreach programmes to empower our local communities and the country at large.

Our Mission

To deliver superior, plant-based cleaning solutions that exceed performance standards while protecting our planet.

Our Values

Prioritizing quality, continuous innovation, sustainability, and absolute customer satisfaction in everything we do.
